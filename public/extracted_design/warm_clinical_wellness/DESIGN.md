## Brand & Style

This design system embodies clinical credibility infused with deep West African warmth and gastronomic heritage. It rejects sterile, detached medical aesthetics in favor of an approach that honors local food traditions—treating regional staples like Ofada rice, Efo Riro, Moi Moi, and Tuwo not as obstacles to health, but as the foundational vehicles for therapy.

### Brand Pillars
- **Culturally Rooted Credibility**: Clinical nutrition advice tailored specifically to Nigerian physiology, ingredients, and lifestyle rather than recycled Western meal plans.
- **Warm Authority**: Scientific rigor delivered with empathy, encouragement, and ancestral familiarity.
- **Nourishing Clarity**: Complex biochemical, caloric, and macro breakdown transformed into intuitive visual aids that empower conscious portioning and ingredient swapping.

### Visual Style
A tailored hybrid of **Tactile Modernism** and **Clean Editorial Health**:
- Substantial, softly curved surfaces inspired by natural calabash and ceramic vessels.
- Deep botanical greens anchored against rich, warm unbleached-linen backgrounds.
- High-contrast, scannable data layouts that prevent health tracking from feeling like a chore.

## Colors

The palette balances the deep medicinal foliage of Nigerian flora (bitter leaf, moringa, scent leaf) with warm staple grains (millet, honey beans, harvest maize).

### Primary Tier
- **Deep Forest Green (`#14432A`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for high-emphasis navigation bars, dominant action triggers, primary headers, and clinical certification stamps. Evokes grounding, medical authority, and vitality.
- **Forest Mid-Tone (`#195333`)**: Used for interactive states, card borders under hover, and secondary headers.

### Secondary Tier
- **Vibrant Growth Green (`#2E8540`)**: Used for affirmative health status indicators, target nutrient achievements, progress bars, active toggle switches, and macro-completion badges.

### Accent & Nutrient Tier
- **Harvest Gold (`#D97706`)**: Clinical alert accents, fiber/micronutrient callouts, streak badges, and portion warning signals.
- **Warm Ochre (`#E5A93C`)**: Star ratings, active glycemic indices, and gentle nutritional highlights.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`)**: Natural unbleached cassava/pap-inspired cream that removes harsh display glare.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white containers for elevated data modules, metric summary cards, and medical logs.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#F4F1EA`)**: Subtly recessed input tracks, empty plate outlines, and macro slot placeholders.

### Neutral & Semantic Tier
- **Charcoal Text Base (`#1F2937`)**: High-contrast, soft-black reading base ensuring accessibility for long dietary notes.
- **Muted Earth (`#6B7280`)**: Secondary metadata, measurement units (g, kcal, ml), and timestamp details.
- **Clinical Crimson (`#DC2626`)**: Critical allergy triggers, hypertensive sodium alerts, and contraindication notices.

## Typography

The pairing combines the approachable geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the unyielding legibility and dense tabular capability of **Inter**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Headlines & Metric Titles**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with negative tracking to keep numbers and Nigerian meal titles (such as *Ofada Brown Rice & Locust Bean Sauce*) tight, impactful, and editorial.
- **Clinical Data & Nutrition Facts**: Set in Inter with tabular figure alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) enabled across all nutritional breakdowns, weights, and lab metric logs to maintain clean column alignment.
- **Labels & Micro-Badges**: Set in Inter Medium and Semi-Bold with subtle positive tracking for instantaneous readability on mobile viewport cards.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first layout prioritizing one-handed thumb interaction during meal preparation, grocery shopping, or clinical dining logging.

### Grid Rules
- **Mobile (<640px)**: 4-column fluid grid. Outside margin of `1rem` (`16px`), gutter `1rem` (`16px`). Bottom-sheet drawers and action bars lock to the lower 35% safe zone.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px)**: 8-column layout. Margin `2rem` (`32px`), gutter `1.5rem` (`24px`). Two-column distribution for Meal Log vs. Daily Metabolic Budget.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1200px`. Margin `3rem` (`48px`), gutter `2rem` (`32px`). Split views keep diet goals fixed alongside active meal building tables.

### Vertical Rhythm
Built on a strict **4px/8px incremental base rhythm**. Padding scales step up logically:
- Internal badge padding: `space-xs` x `space-sm`.
- Card container internal padding: `space-md` on mobile, scaling to `space-lg` on desktop.
- Inter-section stacking: `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **warm, sunlit ambient shadowing** combined with **tonal grounding**, eschewing harsh black shadows that dirty off-white backdrops.

### Shadow Recipe & Depth Levels
Shadows use a warm umber-tinted base derived from soil tones: `rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.06)` to `rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.12)`.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Canvas and inset input tracks (`#F4F1EA`) with no drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card / Log Tile)**: `box-shadow: 0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.03)`. Subtle 1px warm border overlay (`#E8E3D7`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Meal Plate)**: `box-shadow: 0px 8px 20px -4px rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Meal Adjustment Drawer)**: `box-shadow: 0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(20, 67, 42, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(45, 35, 20, 0.06)`. Deep green infusion reinforces brand identity during critical clinical decisions.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry, balancing clinical precision with organic warmth:
- Standard interactive elements (inputs, select menus): `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- Badges and nutrient indicators: Fully curved pill structures (`9999px`).
- Metric modules and nutrition cards: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and prominent hero containers `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to create comforting tactile surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log Meal, Confirm Consultation)**: Background `#14432A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.5rem`, height `48px`. Active press: scales to `0.98` with background `#195333`.
- **Secondary (Swap Ingredient, View Macros)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px` solid `#14432A`, text `#14432A`.
- **Clinical Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#2E8540`, hover background `#F4F1EA`.

### Badges & Macro Chips
- **Calorie Pill**: Background `#F4F1EA`, text `#1F2937`, border `1px` solid `#E8E3D7`. Bold numeric font.
- **Protein Pill**: Background `#DCFCE7` (soft mint), text `#14432A`.
- **Carbohydrate Warning (e.g., Heavy Amala/Garri Portion)**: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, harvest gold border.
- **Therapeutic Stamp (e.g., "Diabetic Safe", "Low Sodium")**: Border radius `9999px`, background `rgba(46, 133, 64, 0.1)`, text `#195333`, font size `11px`, uppercase, bold.

### Food & Ingredient Cards
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` with a `1px` border of `#E8E3D7` and `rounded-xl` corners.
- Left edge features food category tagging (Swallow, Soup, Legume, Grain).
- Displays authentic food labels (*Efo Riro with Smoked Fish*, *Steamed Moi Moi*) paired with serving weights in grams and verified local palm-oil adjustments.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px` solid `#E8E3D7`, text `#1F2937`, height `48px`, border-radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state: border `#2E8540` accompanied by a `3px` focus ring of `rgba(46, 133, 64, 0.15)`. No harsh pure-blue browser defaults.
- Search input includes quick-filter tags for authentic ingredients (e.g., Locust Bean / Iru, Goat Meat, Ugu Leaf, Unpolished Ofada).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkbox: Custom `20px` square, border-radius `4px`, border `2px` solid `#14432A`. Checked state fills `#14432A` with a crisp white tick.
- Segmented Metric Control (Weekly / Monthly / Lab Biomarkers): Contained within a `#F4F1EA` pill track, active pill slides with a `#FFFFFF` elevated card surface.
